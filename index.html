About Us

A community-focused medical centre dedicated to accessible, professional healthcare in Uganda.

Who We Are

Galillee Medical Center is a healthcare facility in Uganda offering medical consultations, pharmacy/dispensary services and supportive care for patients of all ages. We aim to make quality care approachable and trustworthy for the communities we serve.

Our approach is patient-centred: we listen, explain options clearly, and work with you to support your health needs. We emphasise professionalism, respect and continuous improvement in the services we provide.

Privacy & Disclaimer

Privacy Notice

Galillee Medical Center respects your privacy. Information submitted through appointment or contact forms is used solely to respond to your request and provide related services. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing. Sensitive medical details should only be shared during a consultation with a qualified professional.

Medical Disclaimer

Content on this website, including product descriptions, is for general information only and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions regarding a medical condition or medicine. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read here.

Prescription Medicines

Prescription-only medicines are dispensed in accordance with applicable Ugandan regulations and only where a valid prescription or authorised supply is required. Self-medication with prescription products is not recommended.
